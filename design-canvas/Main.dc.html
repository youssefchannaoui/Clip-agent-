What the clipper will look for
…and that is the thing people forget. Allah does not close the door of repentance because you walked through it yesterday. He is not waiting for you to run out of chances.
My mother used to say — and I only understood this much later — His mercy reaches you before you reach Him. That is the whole of it.
So when someone says it is too late, ask them: too late for whom? The door has never once been shut.
